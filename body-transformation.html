How to Progress — Three Levers, In Order
  1. Tension first. Choke up on the same band, or move to a thicker one, before adding anything else.
  2. Then tempo. Slow the lowering/return phase to ~3 seconds on every band movement. This alone makes a light band feel heavy.
  3. Then reps. Once the top of a rep range feels genuinely easy at a given tension, push past it before upgrading the band again.
  4. These three are the whole game without added weight — there's no fourth lever, so don't go looking for one.
  5. Reduce rest time across bursts — 45-60 sec down to 20-30 sec, once tension/tempo/reps have each had their turn.
  6. When 2 sets at the top of a rep range with a slow negative stops being hard, that's the actual signal to increase resistance — not a fixed week number.
  7. Eat well, sleep well, stay consistent — this is 20% of the plan and 80% of the result.
Nutrition Guide — Starting Point
Protein
Lean meat, eggs, fish, beans, Greek yogurt.
Complex Carbs
Rice, oats, potatoes, whole grains.
Vegetables
Eat a real variety, not just one or two.
Fruit
Natural vitamins and fiber — this is where you're starting from, and that's fine.
Healthy Fats
Avocado, nuts, olive oil.
Water
At least 3 litres a day — more on skipping-heavy days.
Sleep
7-9 hours. This isn't optional — recovery is where the transformation actually happens, not the workout itself.
