Long-Term (Months 1–6+)
How to Progress
  1. Increase reps gradually — 1-3 reps per week.
  2. Increase time under tension — slower, more controlled reps.
  3. Reduce rest time over weeks — from 60 sec down to 20-30 sec.
  4. Use harder variations once the current one feels easy.
  5. Track results honestly and aim to beat your own numbers, not anyone else's.
  6. Eat well, sleep well, stay consistent — this is 20% of the plan and 80% of the result.
Nutrition Guide — Starting Point
Protein
Lean meat, eggs, fish, beans, Greek yogurt.
Complex Carbs
Rice, oats, potatoes, whole grains.
Vegetables
Eat a real variety, not just one or two.
Fruit
Natural vitamins and fiber — this is where you're starting from, and that's fine.
Healthy Fats
Avocado, nuts, olive oil.
Water
At least 3 litres a day.
Sleep
7-9 hours. This isn't optional — recovery is where the transformation actually happens, not the workout itself.
