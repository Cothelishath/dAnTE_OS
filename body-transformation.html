How to Progress — Three Levers
  1. Increase tension — choke up on the same band, or move to a thicker one, before adding anything else.
  2. Slow the eccentric — take 3 seconds on the way back/down on every band movement. This alone makes a light band feel heavy.
  3. Increase reps — once 12 feels genuinely easy at a given tension, push to 15 before upgrading the band.
  4. These three are the whole game with no heavy weights — there's no fourth lever, so don't chase one you don't have.
  5. Reduce rest time across bursts — from 60 sec down to 20-30 sec, once tension/tempo/reps have each had their turn.
  6. Track results honestly and aim to beat your own numbers, not anyone else's.
  7. Eat well, sleep well, stay consistent — this is 20% of the plan and 80% of the result.
Nutrition Guide — Starting Point
Protein
Lean meat, eggs, fish, beans, Greek yogurt.
Complex Carbs
Rice, oats, potatoes, whole grains.
Vegetables
Eat a real variety, not just one or two.
Fruit
Natural vitamins and fiber — this is where you're starting from, and that's fine.
Healthy Fats
Avocado, nuts, olive oil.
Water
At least 3 litres a day — more on skipping-heavy days.
Sleep
7-9 hours. This isn't optional — recovery is where the transformation actually happens, not the workout itself.
